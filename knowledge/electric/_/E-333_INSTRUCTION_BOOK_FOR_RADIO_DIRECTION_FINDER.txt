Installation

The bearing accuracy of the direction finder is affected by metal
structures near the loop antenna. Especially in high frequency bands such
as the 2 MHz band, the accuracy is particularly affected by metal
structures and cranes toward the bow.

To minimize bearing error, consider the followings when selecting the loop

antenna installation site.

1. Selecting loop antenna installation site
a) Install the loop antenna on the radar mast, referring to Fig. a or

Fig. b.

When installing on a new-building ship built on or after Feb. 1995
and not requiring direction finding in the 2 MHz band, the loop
antenna may be installed on the compass deck., However, compass deck
installation may cause a larger bearing error than installation on the
top of the radar mast.

4 loop antenna installed on the compass deck should be positicned

approximately 2 m above the deck, utilizing a mast.

to the extent that the depressien angle is equal to or greater than
the angle specified in the INMARSAT-C installation manual.

The loop antenna installed near the MF / HF antenna should be
installed at least 2 m higher than the MF / HF antenna. If the loop
antenna cannot be installed at least 2 m higher than the MF /- HF
antenna, it should be positioned over 4 m distant horizontally from

the MF / HF antenna.
