7.3.7. Change Address

6. Select 7 — Change Address

7. Select the appropriate Loop
(in this example Loop B) and
then enter 255 for Point.

Note: All new detectors come
with a default address of 255.
Therefore this is the address to
be changed.

8. Enter the original address of
the faulty detector as the
New Address (i.e. 006 in this
example).

9. Press F5 (Enter). Display
will show ‘Successful’

10. Press F1 (Back) to clear the
display and then Reset the
panel to clear the ‘No
Response’ and ‘Unconfigured
Device' faults.

Disclaimer: The screenshots shown are taken from MX version 8.1 firmware. Actual
