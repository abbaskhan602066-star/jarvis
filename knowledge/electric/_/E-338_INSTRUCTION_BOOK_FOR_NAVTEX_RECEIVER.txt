11 INTRODUCTION

This manual covers the functions, operation and installation of the NCR-300A NAVTI
receiver.

The NCR-300A has been designed to automatically receive Navigational and Metearo-
logical warnings and urgent information and to print out with the built-in printer,

The NCR-300A is compact and can be manufactured at a low cos
be installed in any type of ship easily.

NAVTEX means the system for the broadcast and automatic reccption of marine safety
information by means of narrow-band direct printing Lelegraphy.

NAVTEX is a component of IHO/IMO world-wide navigational warning service
{WWNWS) defined by IMO Assembly resolution A.419 (XI), and NAVTEX has hcen in-
cluded as an element of the (lobal Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) which
will be implemented from 1st FEB. 1992.

Tn the GMDSS, NAVTEX receiving capability will become part of the mandatory cquip-
ment which is required to be carried in certain vessels under the provision of the revised
chapler IV of the 1974 SOLAS convention from 1st AUG. 1983.
