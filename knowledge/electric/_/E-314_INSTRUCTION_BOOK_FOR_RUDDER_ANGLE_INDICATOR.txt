1. General

This instrument indicates the angle as well as the direction of
rudder of ship by means of pointing needle and calibrated dial
installed in the wheel house or elsewhere, which is a important
instrument for the operation of ships.

" The mechanical position is converted to the electrical signal by
means of synchro-motor in order to operate the indicator.

The indicator has dial calibrated in 4 times of actual rudder
angle (standard is 40° - 0° - 40°), thus the angle on the dial
is 160° - 0° ~ 160°. A pointing needle indicates the rudder
angle and its direction on the dial. A transmitter is mechani-
cally connected to the tiller. The transmitter and the indica—
tor installed elsewhere are connected by means of electric wires,
and the angle of rudder is transmitted to the indicator without
a moment of delay.

In case of three face type, the gear ratio of transmitter is 1:3
to multiply the rudder angle by 3, thus the angle on the dial is
120° - 0° - 120°.

Each parts are specially selected for use on board ship and is
made according to the JIS specifications, the performance of
which is excellent.

3. Principle of operation

The both transmitter and indicator are based upon the theory of
synchro-motor, the principle of which is explained below.

The synchro-motor consists of primary and secondary windings,
which are the rotor as well as stator coils. The terminals of
transmitter is connected to that of indicator correspondingly.
The primary coil is connected to the power source (Rl and R2)
and forms a predetermined magnetic fields. The secondary wind-
ing of the tramsmitter is connected to the corresponding coil of
indicator for each phase (S1, 52 and s3).

When the relative position of the primary and secondary coils is
the same as in the indicator, the voltage induced in the second-
ary coils in the transmitter and the jindicator are same and
there is no current flow. Transmitter Indicator

Thus the synchro follows, and from theoretical point of view
there should be no difference in the angle indication between the
transmitter and indicator, but because of some friction in the
moving parts and accuracy of machining etc., there is some error.
This error is the accuracy (error in the indication.)

4. Mechanism
4-1. Transmitter

Splashproof construction, flat position type, made of aluminum
alloy casting. A driving system is fitted on the top. The
ériving shaft fitted on the top cover and cut in square to in-

‘ stall the driving system. The gears fitted inside multiply the
motion of tiller by 4 times (gear ratio is 1:4). The gear on the
driving shaft consists of two gears with springs acting to pre-
vent the back lash of the gears. Thus the driving gear is meshed
with driven gear of the synchro without any play.

As explained in the General, when three face type indicator
(3L200 type) is used, the angle of tiller is multiplied by three.

The synchro-metor is fitted by means screw onto the bracket and

2 pinion is fitted in the end of the shaft. The bracket is fit-
ted onto the top cover together with driving mechanism. When the
top cover is removed, everything including the synchro can be
taken out. A terminal board contained in terminal box with gland
nut for cable can be opened by loosening 4 screws. The cable
should be connected to the specified terminal as numbered.

(SP200, SL200, SE200, SDL50, SL150, SEL50)

loosening the screw by a screw driver in order to change the
pointing angle. The color of the needle is white when the dial
is black and black when the dial is white.

The light bulb is mounted on a special double ring socket to-
gether with holder. The bulb can be easily changed.

The indicator with light comtrol has rheostat contained in z case
mounted on the right side of the indicator case.

The light can be controlled by rotating the rheostat

plate is fitted under the dial to illuminate the whole dial.

4-2-5. Three face type indicator

This type is a dripproof construction to be mounted on ceiling
of wheel house. It is possible to read the indication from the
starboard, port and front (three directions). The material is
aluminum alloy casting. The cover is a square, transparent
plastic and is designed for easy reading from wide angle.

In order to install this indicator, remove the screw for the
bottom plate and remove the plastic cover, after which put the
bolt in the four corners of the fitting base and tihgten to the
ceiling surface. The synchro-motor is fitted inside of the fit-
ting base. The weight of the synchro being about 12 kg in total,
be careful not to damage the dial and pointing needle etc.

Three synchros are fitted on each face, and are connected in
parallel. The construction of each synchro are same as any other
type, alsc wires can be connected to the each terminal in the
same manner as in the one synchro type.

The illuminating device can be either EL plate or electric light
bulb. The light controller can be installed separately.
