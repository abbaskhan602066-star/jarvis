1. OUTLINE

This windmill type wind indicator ( anemometer/anemoscope ) with wind speed
/ wind direction indicators consists of a transmitter and a receiver.

When instailed on board a ship, the unit measures relative wind direction
and relative wind speed for indication. ( When used on land, where the unit
is at a stand still at all times, the direction and speed of wind indicated
on the unit can be regarded as the true wind direction and velocity. )

Calculations to obtain true ( to the ground ) wind direction and speed from
the relative values are generally done using the calculation board.

For detail on how to use the calculation board, refer to the attached inst-
ruction manual.

The transmitter sends out the wind direction / speed signals to the recei-
ver. The receiver, receiving those signals fed from the transmitter, indi-
cates the wind direction and the wind speed values. They are connected with
cables.

3.2 Wind Direction

AC

An AC synchro is used for measuring wind direction, It utilizes the
property of the synchro that can convert the mechanical changes in the
angles into the corresponding electrical signals for the telemetric
indication of the measured values, The synchro is composed of the single
-phase primary winding { the rotor ) and the 3-phase secondary winding
( the stator ).

When the shaft of the synchro at the transmitter side is oriented in
the same direction as that of the shaft of the synchro at the receiver
side, the value of voltage induced af the synchro at the transmitter
side is egual to the voltage to be induced in the one at the receiver
side, hence no current is allowed to flow in the secondary winding.

( Refer to Fig 2 ) Therefore, no rotating force is created in this case.
If the shaft of the synchro at the tramsmitter side is oriented in the
direction different from the direction of the shaft of the synchro at
the receiver side, then, the voltage induced at the former is not equal
to the voltage induced in the latter, hence current is allowed to flow
in the secondary winding. ( Refer to Fig.3)

As a result, a strong rotating force is created in the receiver due to
such current flowing in the secondary winding, creating a forque which
acts to bring its shaft to the same direction as that of the receiver
comes to a stop ab the point where it comes to align with the shaft of
the transmitter, Through the follow-up motion of the synchro at the
receiver side to thst of the one at the transmitfer side, both the
transwitter and the receiver align with each other.

Transmitter

External view of the transmitter is shown below.

A propeller is attached to its leading edge to measure wind speed, and
a tail unit is provided at ist tail end to make the umit follow up the
stream of wind more easily.

The case is designed to be watertight. A terminal box is incorporated
at the lower part of the transmitter, and the connection cable is led in
through the hole in the terminal box cover.

The balance-control function is provided to prevent potential swaying
of the shell due to the roiling of the vessel when installed on a ship.
If incorporates an AC ganerator generating wind-speed signals and a
synchro which generates wind-direction signals,

The generator protected with its casing, and its shaft is supported
with ball bearing, and is attached with a propeller at its leading edge.
To allow measurement of even low-speed bleeze, no iron core is used for
the stator of the generation mechanism, and electrical frictions are
held to the minimum, The electromotive force created in the genmerator is
led to the terminal board at the lower part via the slip-ring.

Shall of the transmitter can be rotated freely. The bottom end of its
rudder shaft is connected with the shaft of the wind-direction measuring
synchro via a coupling, The synchro itself is fixed to the casing.

5.

1

Attaching Propellsr

In some cases, the propeller for the transmitter may has been detached
from the body and shipped in a separate package to prevent it from dama-
go during transport. In such a case, attach the propeller to the trams-
mitter bofore use. Care should be taken as the profile of the propaller-
mounting face may differ somewhat from type to type of the transmitier.

Referring to the illustration below, set the propeller correctly to the
propeller shaft so that it can fit in the groove in the shaft snugly,
and fasten it in place firmly by tightening the set screw.

CAUTION :

When attaching the propelier, be careful not to bend the shaft. If the
shaft is bent, it will not only be unable to indicate correct wind speed
but also may results in a fajlure in the generator.

5.2 Instatlation of the Transmitter

Install the transmitter with the " BOW * mark in its flange directed
correctly to the bow of the vessel. If it ts installed in the wrong
direction, wind direction cannot be indicated correctly.

CAUTLON :

(1) Select a suitable place for installation, whwre the transmitter
is subject to natural wind flows, being clear of any obstacles
that can obstruct the natural flows of wind. If there is an
obstacle naer the transmitter, it is necessary to keep it away
from such an obstacle at least two meters. or so.

In marine use, the transmitter is generally installed on the
radar mast. In this case, consideration should be made to avoid
turbulence of wind that may be caused by the rotary object like
the radar scanner, ect.

{2) The transmitter is so manufactured that it can follow up even a

~  low-speed bleeze, and also it has been balance-adjusted relative
to the movements of the rotary shaft, Therefore, it is free of any
abnormal rotations due to the rolling or pitching of the vessel.
If the tramsmitter should rotate lightly, the installation place
is not properly selected.

(3) Though it is designed to be earthquake-proof, select a place of
least vibration for instaliation,

(4) Avoid installing it near the high-output radio antenna. Otherwise
» there may be a possibility of the indication of wind speed may
change suddently.

5.3 Connection

Arrange wiring between the transmitter and the receiver using shielded
cable { 1,25 mm squ. ), according to the wiring diagram.

Good care should be take to aveid errarneous wiring: there may he a
danger of damaging the wind-speed indicator if a commercial power supply
is applided by mistake to the wind-speed indicator circuit.

Also, adequate care should be taken in wiring to keep away sufficiently
gom other high-voltage circuit or a circuit in which a large current

ows.
