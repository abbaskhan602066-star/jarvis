3. CONSTRUCTION
3.1 General

The battery shall consist of the posilive and negative plates, separators, cover and
container, etc. each of which is of high quality materials, and shall be durable for
long term use. Further, the actual profile of the battery shall confornm ta that as

3.2 Normal Seal
The battery shall provide a fully sealed system escaping in minimum free gas evolved
within the cell by recombination of oxygen evolved at the positive plate, with the
lead sponge in the negative plate.

3.3 Plales
Both of positive and negative plates are of a pasted type assuring uniform quality in
all the surfaces. They are free from gird breakage (fracture), active material cracks
or shedding and plate buckling or bulging having any detrimental effect on the cell
performance.

3.4 Separators

A non-woven micro-fiber glass separator shall be placed between the positive and
negative plates as an electrolyte absorbing and retaining separator.

3.5 Covers and Containers

The cover and container shall be ruggedly built with high quality synthetic resin and
shall be well insulated.

3.6 Blectrolyte

The electrolyte shall conform to dilute acid as specified in JIS K 1321
(Sulfuric Acid).

3.7 Visual Appearance
The battery appearance shall be {ree from cxcessive dirt, distortion and seepage.
