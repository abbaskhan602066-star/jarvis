3. Mechanism and Operation

3-1. Electric Window Wiper

The electric motor, crank mechanism, shaft bearings, con-
densers, terminal board, are installed in the main body of

this window wiper.

The principle of operation is that the electric motor drives
the rotating disc and the rotary movement is changed to
reciprocating motion by means of crank mechanism.. This
reciprocating motion conveyed by the shaft from the rear
side of the main body drives the arm on which the blade is

nounted.

The arm head is fitted on the end of the shaft by a bolt.
In case of outside fitting, a sub-arm is fitted on the

bearing case with sub-arm pin. In case of inside fitting,

a plate is installed on the outside of the fitting surface
of the wiper. The sub-arm pin is fitted onto this plate,
and the sub-arm is fitted on this pin. The arm and sub-

arm are connected with the fitting bolt at the blade.

The purpose of the sub-arm is to let the blade swing in

parallel fan-type motion.

Synthetic rubber is used for the blade touching the glass,
and the blade arm is spring loaded to obtain good contact
of the blade to the glass. Adjust this spring to get

proper contact pressure.

5. Maintenance and Checking, Replacement Parts
5-1. Motor and Speed Reducer

A radial ball bearing is used in the motor shaft as well as
the speed reducer shaft. These ball bearings are sealed.
The speed reducer is filled with grease, therefore it is
not necessary to apply oil to these parts.

When it has become necessary t0 replace the motor znd speed
reducer, open the cover of the wiper's main body ahd re-
move the link rod from the disc, and also remove the motor
together with the fitting base. The speed xeducer motor

is an integral type and is fitted onto thé fitting base

with 4 bolts.

5-2. Condenser

Open the cover of the wiper's main body and remove the f£it-~
ting bolts of the condenser for replacement. Check the

capacity of the condenser when replacing.

5-3. Link Rod (Available as spare parts

The bearing of this part is made of special alloy for bear-
ings. The sliding surface is mirror finished to get good
contact. Grease is filled in the cover so that oiling is
not necessary. The reason for replacing may be that the
matal fatigued after a long use. When replacing the link
rod, loosen the screw of the crank disc as well as the

screw of the link.

--- PAGE 22 ---
5-4. Arm and Blade (Blade is available as spare parts

The oil-less metal is used in all bearings of the arm that
are exposed on the outside. The casting parts are made o
brass and other parts are made of stainless steel.

It is normally not necessary to replace the arm, but when
it does become necessary to replace the arm, remove it

from the shaft by loosening the tightening bolt.

The blade is made of good quality rubber with good anti-
weather characteristics. By loosening the Fitting screws,
the rubber blade can be removed.

5-5. Speed Regulator (EWP—ZGS, EWP-26SH, EWP—ZSS/D)
EWP-36S, EWP-36SH, EWP-365/D

Semiconductors are used for this regulator, and they can

be used semipermanently, unless there is a mistake in wiring
or violent vibrations/shocks.. However, when replacing
becomes necessary, remove the protector and pull it out from
the socket. In case of DC 24V, loosen the nut and replace
the whole unit, and avoid mistakes when rewiring.

5-6. Heater and Thermostat (EWP-2GH, EWP—ZESH,)
EWP-36H, EWP-36SH

The heater and thermostat which are made of high corrosion
resistant stainless steel are properly insulated, and are
constructed to last.

In order to avoid high temperatures during prolonged use,
the thermostat is used to prevent insulation deterioration,
as well as maintaining safety in running.

When replacing the cover after maintenance work, close it

tightly to prevent water seepage.

1. General

This Electric Horizontal Window Wiper is to wipe off rain water
or splash of sea water on the front window glass of the steer~
ing house, etc. in order to maintain a clear view for safe
watchout and navigation.

This device is designed so that the wiper arm (blade} moves
horizontally by converting unilateral rotary motion of the
motor into linear reciprocal motion by means of the belt and
the crank mechanism.

3-1. Electric horizontal window wiper

The main body consists of the motor unit,. casing, and wiper
arm with blade.

3-1-1. Motor unit

The motor unit consists of the motor, reduction gears, tacho-
generator, and terminal board. The unit is of waterproof
structure and at the end of its output shaft is fitted a
pulley (for outside fitting type) or coupling (for inside
fitting type).

An electronically controlled induction motor with rated output
of 70W is used to allow speed control. This unit is fitted to
the wiper main body with four fitting bolts.

3-1-2, Casing (See Figs. 1 and 2.)

The casing portion is composed of the main body case, belt,
carriage plate, pulleys and rod.

For the case, corrosion-resistant aluminium casting material
(A5052S) is used., The case also serves as a guide rail on
which the carriage plate runs.

The rod, which is centered on the four roller carriage plate,
is fixed to one point on the belt which extends between the
driving and driven pulleys.

This rod converts the rotary motion of the belt into recipro-
cal motion, which causes the carriage plate to move between
the upper and lower guide rails reciprocally.

This motion is transmitted via the arm head and the arm to
the blade, which thus wipes off rain/sea water.

In the case of a two-blade type, the above-mentioned motion
of the carriage plate is transmitted to an additional cax-
riage plate. The method of transmission is as follows:

A pair of pulleys is fitted outside of the aforementioned
driving and driven pulleys, and the belt extended between
these pulleys connects the two carriage plates. Thus, the
driving carriage plate drives the additional carriage plate.

3-1-3. Wiper arm

The wiper arm which consists of the arm head, arn and blade
is mounted to extend downward from the carriage plate.
Synthetic rubber is fitted on the blade and an adjustable
spring is provided in the bent portion between the arm head
and the arm so that the rubber contacts the glass evenly.

Adjust the blade pressure with this spring, as the force
applied to the glass surface changes depending on the rela-
tions between the wiper fitting surface and the window glass
surface.

3-1-4. Heater (Optional)

During navigation in cold weather, ice often forms between the
carriage plate and the casing, making the use of the wiper
impossible, Therefore, a heater is provided to melt the ice
in the casing.

This heater is designed exclusively for the wiper and is not
for window glass. Note that this heater i1s not effective when
the blade is stuck frozen to the window glass.

3-2. Switch box

The switch box contains the speed regulator, fuses, toggle
switches, capacitor, terminal board, etc.

The speed regulator carries out no-stage control of wiper
speed within the range of 0 A maximum (which varies depending
on types). By turning the speed regulator knob, the motor
input is controlled for speed regulation.

To stop the operation of the wiper, turn off the power switch
{toeggle switch). When the switch is turned "ON", power shall
be turned on to operate wiper.

Turning the speed regulator knob to "HIGH" side will increase
the blade speed, and turning it to "LOW" side will decrease
the speed.

The position of the knob can be selected without stages
between "LOW" and "HIGH". A desired position fitting the
condition of rain/sea water on the glass window can be
selected.

To stop the blade at a certain position, set the speed at
"LOW" and turn “OFF" the power switch when the blade comes to
the desired position.

When the wiper is not in use, be sure to turn "OFF" the power
switch.

The operation of the wiper on a sunny day or when the window
glass is dry, the motor may overload to cause troubles. In
such case, sprinkle water on the glass surface while operating
the wiper.

If a heater is mounted, turn "ON' the heater switch (toggle
switch) which will turn on power for the heater with simulta-
neocus lighting of the heater indicator lamp.

Although a thermostat is incorporated near the heater, be sure
to turn "OFF" the heater switch when the wiper begins to
operate normally.
