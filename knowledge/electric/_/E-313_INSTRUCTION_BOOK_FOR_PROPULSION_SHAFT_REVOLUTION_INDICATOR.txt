Operation

This revolution counter is set to zero before shipping, and
when the power is turned on, “0" will be displayed.

As the engine is put in opration, the counter counts up one by
one at every revolution. At the same time, external output
signals (F1 to E4) are delivered m;r'm'ding to the setting of
DIP switch. The maximum reading is “99998999” , which then
goes back to zero.

If the power supply of the counter is cut off due to accident
or other reason, the count of revoltions immediately before
failure is stored in the memory element, and when the power is

recovered, the counting is resumed [rom the iatrrupted moment.

Measurement of average speed of revolution (Type: RE-S8AR,
RE-S8DR only. )

This counter can measure the average speed of revolution in

I to 5 minutes without affecting the total counting of the num-
ber of revolutions. This measuring method is explained below.
When tha AVERAGE RPM button is pressed while the engine is run-
ning, the display flickers at “00000000” to tell that the measu-
rement has begun. In this state, the measurement cannot be sto-

pped for at léast for a minute.

--- PAGE 43 ---
When one minute has passed, the flickering display changes to
“11111111" and it changes to "222222" and “"33333333” and so
forth at every minute. That is, the flickering numeral indi-
cates the lapse of time from start. For instance, the flicker-
ing “33333333" means threc minutes have passed from the start.
The maximum measuring duration is b minutes, and Lhe measurement
is stopped automatically, and the average spead of revolution in
the past five minutes is displayed for 30 seconds, and then the
display returns to the original revolution counting. When the
AVERAGE RPM button is pressed while the average specd is being
displayed, the display returns to the revolution counting.

When the AVERAGE RPM button is pressed while the display is
flickering with numeral 1 to 4, the measurement stops, and the
average speed al that time is displayed for 30 seconds, then
the display returns to the original revalution counting.

_ Similarly, when the AVERAGE RPM button is pressed during the
speed display, it returns to the revoution counting.

The average speed is displayed to the first decimal by rounding
off the second decimal: for example, 1213.4 (rpm).

If revolution signals are supplied only for 10 seconds during
measurement, it is stopped, and the display returns to the ori-
ginal revolution counting.

If AVERAGE RPM button is pressed while the engine is stopped,
“00000000” is displayed for 10 seconds, and the display returns
to the revolution counting.

When Lhe average speed is measured while the revolution is slow,
being below 30 rpm, it may Lake a slightly longer time until
the speed is displayed after the measuremnt is stopped.

(For example, at 20 rpm, the maximum Lime is 3 seconds.)
