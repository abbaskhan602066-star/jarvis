‘This instrument is used for vessels navigating through the Suez Canal at night,
and is designed and manufactured so as to comply with the Suez Canal Navigation Rule
In order to meet the capacity required by the Rule, full consideration is made in
such details as optical efficiency, vaterproofness, anti-corrosiveness,
virbration-proofness and operational capacity.
Furthermore, in order to make it moved or conveyed more easily, it is small-sized
. and |ight-weighted.
For the sake of exploiting the capacity of this instrument more surely, you are

cordially solicited to use it after reading this instruction.

1 Installatlon - of SearchHilght
O Whenever a Suez Canal Searchlight is installed aboard a carrier of petroleum,
LPG, LNG or other inflammable material, or a vessel from which gas has not been

freed, install it at a well-ventilated, safe location avoiding dangerous areas.
