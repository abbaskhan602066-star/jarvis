Precautions

Please refer to attached "Reference Book" as for installation, specification and general
description of the JAX-90 facsimile.

About Power Source

The JAX-90 has the 100V AC and 220V AC (50/60Hz) power supply systems.
The 100V AC system allows connection of 80 to 132V AC while the 220V AC
system allows connection of 160 to 264V AC. Whenever connecting the unit to
the power line, be sure to ascertain the connection of short bar.

When not Using of Equipment for a Long Time )

During recording, the thermal head is pushed
onto the rubber roller with a considerable force. RECORD
At this time, the mechanical switch is put to the
RECORD]| position. If the facsimile is left
unusedforalong time with the mechanical switch
putto the [RECORD | position, the rubber roller
will be deformed due to pressure applied to it. If
you are going to leave the facsimile unused for
more than one day, be sure to put the mechanical
switch to the m position. Be sure to
pull the paper evenly before resetting the me-
chanical switch to the [RECORD | position. RELEASE

Entry of foreign object into mechanical parts

On the thermal head, a small resistor (heating element) is protected with a film.
If a hard foreign object should stick to the recording parts, the film protecting the
heating element would be damaged, resulting in partial destruction of the heating
element, and missing of printing. Avoid dust and dirt. Never grind the heating
element (tip) on the thermal head with a file or a screwdriver.

--- PAGE 29 ---
Avoid direct sunlight

The JAX-90 employs the photo-sensor and thermo sensitive recording paper. Be
careful about the place of installation of the JAX-90. Otherwise, it is possible that
the irregular reflection and heat of direct sunlight would cause malfunction of the
JAX-90 or coloring of recording paper.

Prevention of electrical shock

Even when the power switch is turned off, voltage continues to be applied to some
parts for backup of the memory and timer. Whenever you are going to check the
interior of the JAX-90, switch off the AC mains beforehand. (Input line)

Setting Receiving Frequency

To receive the signal by the JAX-90, the frequency should be set. This section
describe the receiving frequency.

The receiver in the JAX-90 can receive the long wave (80-160kHz) and short wave (2-
25MHz) FAX broadcasting.

Each broadcasting station transmits several frequencies at a time. Selection of a proper
frequency affects the quality of the picture. However, it is difficult to select a proper
receiving frequency because the conditions of propagation vary with seasons, daytime,
night, and receiving points. Therefore, you should always bear in mind how to select
a proper receiving frequency.
