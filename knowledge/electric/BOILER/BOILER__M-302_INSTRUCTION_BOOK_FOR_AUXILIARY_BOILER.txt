Out-line of Burner

This burner consists of rotary-cup, Wind-box, Ignition system and safety
control devices for combustion. The fuel oil is led to rotary-cup burner
and it is spread-out inside of rotary-cup. By high speed rotation of
rotary-cup, fuel oil is spread-out equally, and it makes very thin oil
film from cup-edge to 90 degree angle.

High speed air from primary air nozzle rotates opposite direction of
rotary-cup collides into thin oil film and it makes atomization.
Secondary air (For combustion) is distributed equally by wind-box and it
is supplied from out-side of primary air nozzle for easy mixing of oil
particles.

Ignition is made by flame of diesel oil pilot burner. Atomization of
main burner is done around rotary-cup. Therefore, oil pressure is
relatively low and atomization is equal. And complete combustion and

low load combustion with excess air rate can be done

Preparation for Operation

a. Water and solid including waste oil may cause problems for oil pump
operation or cloging of strainer. Of corse, combustion becomes poor

and cause black smoke or un-burned waste oil may stick to the furnace
wall.

Therefore, enough drainage id done in waste oil service tank and
clean strainer proir to operation.

b. Waste oil has characteristics for not easy flow when waste oil temp.
is low and it is very difficult for atomization.
In case waste oil is bellow flash point, it never be burned.

Therefore, waste oil is heated proir to combustibn.

Heating temperature is normally 80 to 100 degree C but necessity of
waste oil heating is depend on condition of waste oil.

Please adjust by combustion condition. Of corse, water content can
be evaporated by heating.

c. Even water is drained from waste oil which has some water content.
And it is very easy to make layer of water and oil by gravity and
temperature difference in waste oil service tank. Before you supply
to burner, waste oil is enough agitated in order to make equal
quality and water evaporation is also easy.

. Operation

After completion of pre-operation check, burner is stopped momentary
and three(3) way valve unit is changed over to waste oil combustion and
operate waste oil pump.

Itis also necessary to change oil pressure by characteristics of waste
oil. 1In case waste oil has low carolific value, oil pressure is
decreased and oil flow is increased.

In case waste oil has high carolific value, oil pressure is decreased

and oil flow is finally decreased in order to prevent black smoke or
flame failure.

. Stop

For stopping, burner is stopped and waste oil pump is also stopped.
Suction line of waste oil is changed to diesel oil, and then waste oil
pump is operated 2 or 3 minutes.

After waste oil line is changed to diesel oil, 3-way valve unit is
canged over C heavy oil.

CONSTRUCTION AND FEATURES

This valve is a oil flow control valve for proportional cosbustion control of oil burner.
When it is used in cosbustion with proportional pressure controller and control motor, it
controls Flow amount of oil in proportion to stean pressure of the boiler.

The first advantage of the valve is that ; it is provided with three special Flow amount
adjusting mechanisas, one of thea is a spindle () which rotates according to an operation
of a control wotor. Such flow amount adjusting mechanisas make it possible to change opti-
onally flow characteristics of the valve, thus maintain an air-fuel ratio fitted for any
air supply characteristics.

FLON_AMOUNT ADJUSTING MECHANISK

Adjustaent of flow amount is made by changing an opening area between the spindle and a
sleeve (2) as the spindle has a rotational and reciprocating motion.
Flow amount adjustment is made in following manner.

1) Adjustaent by a rotation of the spindle

The spindle is connected to control motor and flow amount is adjusted proportionally
according to a change of load. Rotation angle of the spindle is max. 100° between 0
to 10 positions of thrust cam scale. When the spindle is turned toward scale position
10 from 0, Flow amount decreases.

2) Adjustment by an. adjusting bolt

By turning the adjusting bolt (3) , a clearance betueen spindle (cutted portion) and
sleeve can be changed. When the adjusting bolt is screwed-in, flow amount of the
valve increases and flow charactristics is also changed.

3) Adjustaent by an adjusting screw (thrust cam mechanisa)
Flow anount can be 2djusted more strictly with adjusting screw (¥) of the thrust cas
in accordance with an air supply charactristics of burner. When the adjusting screw
are screwed in, flow amount of the valve increases.

I. MOUNTING THE FIXED LEVEL CONDENSER
Mounting Position

In order to detect the water level of drum by com-
paring the level head inside the condenser with that of the
drum , the condenser should be mounted so that it will
maintain a fixed level of water.

To prevent erroneous indication arising from change of
relative position between drum , water level and the fixed
level, the change resulting from inclination of ship's hull
(heal trim), it is desirable to mount the condenser above
the center of the drum water surface.

As shown by Fig. 5 & 6, it is necessary that dimension
of R toward stern should be less than 1500mm, and toward

both sides of the ship should be made shortest.

2. Piping Works

2-1 Piping to condenser should be in rising gradient or
horizontal straight piping. However, as shown by Fig. 4,
the dimension A should be less than 400mm, If it exceeds
LOOmm, or if bend is used, downcomer should be provided.

2-2 In case of length of condenser piping is over 400mm,

lagging should be provided to decrease amount of condensation.

3-1 In case of glove valve is used on the condenser side as a
shut-off valve as shown by Fig.3, its handle should be fitted
facing sideways to provide smooth return of drain to drum

.

because if it is fitted turning upward, drain may fill to the

--- PAGE 77 ---
condenser giving rise to a change of water level position
with subsequent errorneous indication.

3-2 In case of the shut-off valve on condenser side is of
elbow type valve as shown by Fig. 1 and 2, the flange on the
outlet side should be fitted to face sideways or upward,
because if it turns downward drain may be collected inside the

condenser.

II MOUNTING THE DIFFERENTIAL PRESSURE TRANSMITTER

l. Mounting Position and Mounting Method

1-1 The transmitter should be mounted lower than the shut-
off valve on the water side and close to the drum as much as
practicable so as to facilitate adjustment.

In order to minimize the error due to rolling of the
ship, it should be mounted so that its front faces to ship's
side.

1-2 In order to secure the transmitter free from vibration
of low frequency (less than 10HZ), it should be mounted on a

sturdy stand or on pipe of S50A.

IIT PIPING WORK OF DIFFERENTIAL PRESSURE CONDUIT
1. Connect the conduit (15A) on fluctuating level side

(water side) with HP side of the differential transmitter.
Also connect conduit (15A) on fixed level side (steam side)

with LP side.

--- PAGE 78 ---
2. Each conduit on HP and LP side should be installed
parallel to prevent error due to difference of ambient
temperature.

3. 1In order to prevent air pocket generating in the conduits,
care must be taken to install each conduit with inclination

of more than 1/10 up to the differential transmitter.

4. To assimilate the ambient temperature, each conduit should

not be lagged.

IV. PREPARATION FOR OPERATION

1. Sealing Conduit against Water

In sealing the conduit, care must be taken so that no air
is allowed to remain in the line.

Difference of temperature between HP and LP conduits
may result in difference of specific gravity leading to wide
error in level indication.

Before sealing conduits, remove the differential trans-
mitter from manifold valve and fill the djaphragm chambers on
both the HP and LP sides with water to extract air. Upon
restored, perform sealing of the line.

1-1 Sealing Method after Filling Water to drum.

Open the HP and LPside valves as well as the pressure
equalizing valve of the differential pressure transmitter.
Open the respective shut-off valve attached to drum on
steam and water sides,

After allowing them to stand under this state for few

hours, close the shut-off valves on steam and water side and

--- PAGE 79 ---
also close the pressure equalizing valve, then fill the water
by removing plug on the top of condenser. When water is
filled to the full, close the plug. Then open each shut-off
valve on steam and water side.
1-2 Sealing method after drum pressure is built up.
Close each shut-off valve attached to drum on steam
and water side, then loosen the bolts of flange of conduit

of the shut-off valve on water side to provide air vent.

Open HP and LP side valves as well as the pressure
equalizing valve of the differential pressure transmitter

and feed the water by removing upper plug of the condenser.

Upon confirming that water is coming out from the conduit

flange of the shut-off valve on water side, tighten the bolts.

When condenser is filled up with water close the plug, and

close the pressure equalizing valve too. Then open each shut-

off valve on both steam and water side.

NOTE: After sealing is done as mentioned above, start adjuste-
ment upon confirming HP, LP and temperature difference
of conduit and that condenser is filled up with water.

2. Operating the draft

2-1 Draft
Air supplied to each instrument should be dry and clean.

After opening drain plugs of reducing valves with filter

attached to both differential pressure transmitter and pressure

regulalator, open the air supply valve to release drain and
others. Then plug up the drain plug and set supply air pres-

sure to 1.4 kg/cm2 (% 0.1 kg/cm?) before starting operation.

3. Setting the Measuring Instrument

3-1 Differential Pressure Transmitter

Although fundamental adjustment of the transmitter had
been made before shipment, if it is necessary to make field
adjustment dependent on mounting position of condenser and other
circumstances, adjustment should be made in accordance with items
of Section V, ADJUSTKENT of the fcllowing page.

When test under attendance is to be made in short
time after completion of aforementioned sealing, or when
there is any discrepancy with plan of condenser, suppression
spring of the transmitter should be adjusted so that output
of the differential pressure transmitter will coincide with
actual water level. Besides, when coincidence with actual
water level is difficult to obtain on account of temperature
difference of conduits or due to residual air, it is necessary
to re-correct actual level (water gauge) and indication under
stabilized condition 2 ~ 3 davs after starting operation of
the drum.

3-2 Pressure Switch

In order that the indication of water level will be
of desired value, supplied air pressure from the differential
pressure transmitter should be adjusted so that the pressure
switch will operate at the value obtained by such adjustment.

Besides, ON/OFF performance of the switch should be

confirmed through operating sound, tester and buzzer, etc..

--- PAGE 81 ---
3-3 Pressure Regulator
Setting should be made in reference to normal/reverse
action, proportional action and integral action.
a) Change-over of normal/reverse action
Normal/reverse action is determined by action of
diaphragm valve. Dependent upon change of water level of
, the action in connection with the supplied air

pressure from the differential pressure transmitter is:

rise of level - increase of output - valve closing (air

to shut) - normal action

rise of level - decrease of output - valve closing (air

less shut) - reverse action
In each instances above, turn the proportional band dial and
change the stopper as follows:
Normal action - to lower hole
reverse " - to upper hole
b) Proportional action
In‘fﬁeduatar control, set proportional band (PB) to
about 15 - 40%. )
c) Integral action
Set the reset rate dial to 4 - 0.5 R/M . Besides,

in case of feedwater control, cycling seldom occurs, and hence,

if sharp action is desired (in such case as large size drum )

set PB to 15 and resetting to 4.

4. Cautions during operation

4-1 Water Level Indication
Generally speaking, the output signal (water level indication) of
the differential pressure transmitter varies to some extent with times of
operation, i.e. operation after adjustment and operation in a few hours
afterward starting normal operation. The size and the time required
for stabilization are dependent upon the following factors.

a)  Time required for assimilation of temperature of each conduit
with amb ent temperature,

b)  The size of error dependent upon difference between the gravity
of water in the pipe at the time of adjustment of water gauge and differ-
ential pressure transmitter, and the gravity at normal temperature,

¢)  Amount of air remaining in the pipe and the time of escape.

Accordingly, adjustment of level indication in connection with actual
level should be made, for the best result, at the time when temperature
at each part is stabilized in a few hours after starting normal operation,
Thence no error in indication will occur.

4-2  Confirmation of Pressure Switch Action.

Confirm the actions for each purpose.

Besides, in case timer is assembled in output line, also confirm the
action of timer.

4-3  Pressure Regulator and Diaphragm control valve

a) By raising the water level set point (green indication) of the
pressure regulator, confirm that the diaphragm valve does open thereby.

b) By applying hydraulic pressure with diaphragm valve closed,

check for leakage of water.

1. Adjustment of Range of Differential Pressure Transmitter

In case of standardized instrumentation, measuring range of instru-
ment and suppression, etc. are predetermined, and hence, adjustment of

This manual covers the operation instructions for the PREX3000 Differ-
ential Transmitters. Be sure to read this manual before starting operating
the instrument.

This manual has been written for the instruments of the standard
specifications. Note that some items may differ for the instruments of
modified specifications.

GENERAL PRECAUTIONS

1.  Delivery Check

Upon receipt of the instrument, immediately check it for any damage
which might have been sustained when in transportation. If any signs of
damage to the instrument or missing accessories are found, immediately
notify the transportation company and your Yamatake-Honeywell agent.

2. specification Check

Major specifications are indicated on the nameplate posted on the
instrument case. Check that the specifications conform with the one shown
in the model number table. (When making any enquires on the instrument,
indicate the model number and serial number.)

£ Transportation of Instrument

When transporting the instrument to the place of installation, it is
most recommendable to transport the instrument being protected -in the
packing in the state in which the instrument was delivered, with a view to
prevent damage to the instrument.

4.  Storing of Instrument
(1) Store the instrument in a place where the instrument is not
exposed to rain or other adverse weather or to mechanical
vibration or shocks. Although the "specification" storing

temperature range of the instrument is -40°C to +85°C (that of
the instrument with indicator is 0°C to +55°C), it is most
recommendable to store the instrument at normal temperature and
normal humidity (approximately 25°C and 65% RH).

(2) It is most recommendable to store the instrument in the packing
in the state in which the instrument is delivered to you.

(3) When storing the instrument which has been used, be sure to
drain out liguid from both high and low pressure sides of the
meter body and thoroughly clear the instrument.

(4) When the measured fluid is toxic, handle the instrument care-
fully even after the pressures are released from the instrument.

--- PAGE 96 ---
5. Place of Installation

To let the instrument maintain its performance and reliability for a
long period, pay attention to the following which selecting a place of
installation of the instrument.

1)

(2)

(3)

Ambient Temperature

(a) Select a place where rapid or slow temperature change is
as small as possible.

(b) When it is unavoidable to install the instrument in a
place where it is subjected to radiation from a source of
heat (such as process plant itself), provide a heat
insulator and select a well ventilated location in order
that ambient temperature of the instrument is made as low
as possible.

(c) when there is a possibility of freezing of the measured
fluid, provide a heat insulator or other means to prevent
freezing.

Atmosphere

Avoid, whenever avoidable, installing the instrument in a
corrosive atmosphere.

Vibration and Shock

Select a place where the instrument is not subjected to mecha-
nical vibration or shock.

the following:

(2)

3)

Never apply pressures higher than the rated pressures. (See
Section 1.2.)

Be sure to completely release the pressures form the instrument
before tightening or loosening its clamping-bolts.

Overload Protection Mechanism

Where pressure higher than the measured pressure is applied from the
high-pressure or low-pressure side, the measuring bellows moves and the
movement of the sealing liquid becomes zero between the inside of the
bellows and the high-pressure chamber due to the O-ring seal. After sealing
by the O-ring, over-pressure is received by the whole part of the center
body due to the incompressible nature of the sealing liguid. Consequently,
the measuring bellows etc. are not subjected to unreasonable force.

OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The input for the transmitter is applied as a torque through the
torque tube. The torque causes the gap between the nozzle and flapper to
change. The gap change causes the back pressure of the nozzle to change.
The back pressure is boosted in both pressure and capacity by the pilot
relay in order to be used as an output air pressure.

The output air pressure of the pilot relay is converted into a mecha-
nical force by the feedback bellows. The mechanical force is fed as vector
Fl to the beam through the strap as shown in Figure 8, thereby making up a
negative feedback loop to balance the output air pressure at a valve propor-
tional to the input.

For elevation (or suppression), a force is applied to the beam by the
elevation (or suppression) spring.

Span change an be accomplished by changing the direction of vector F3.
As the direction of vector F3 is changed, the effective force exercised by
vector F1 changes and consequently the feedback gain changes, thereby
realizing span change.

When selecting a place of installation for the instrument, take into
consideration the matters related to instrument inspection, maintenance,
longevity, and operation safety as follows:

(1)  select a place where temperature change is small (within the

heat.

cause damage to the meter body. Provide appropriate means to
guard against freezing.

vibration.

OPERATION METHOD

The meter body, together with the transmitter coupled to it, starts
operating as the air supply and process input are fed to it. (It is recom-
mendable to check the operation of the transmitter before starting the
running operation.)

1; Differential Pressure (Flow) Transmission

The operation method (start up procedure) here is described assuming
that manifold valves and below piping are prepared as shown in Section 5.1
of Part "INSTALLATION." Of Model 71 or 72, operation is ready when its
installation is over.

(1) Check that piping is correctly done.

(2)  Unless self drain or self vent piping is done, liquid or gas
will be entrapped in the measuring chambers and pressure connec-
tion pipes, thereby causing measurement errors. Be sure to
drain out or vent out such liquid or gas. The same applied
regardless of whether manifold valves are used or manifold
piping is done. For the procedure, refer to Part "INSPECTION
AND MAINTENANCE.

(3)  Zero point check:

When the instrument has become the measuring state, set a
receiver instrument and check and adjust the zero point as
follows: Of the manifold valves, close the stop valves of both
high and low pressure sides and then open the equalizer valve.
Next, open the stop valve of the high pressure side alone so
that the process pressure of the high side alone is applied to
both high and low pressure chambers of the meter body. Under
this state, check the zero point. After the zero point check is
complete, close the equalizer valve and then open the stop valve
of the low pressure side.

(4) Operating procedure: (Refer to Section 9.1)
To start operating the meter body from the state that all valves
are closed, open or close them in the due order as noted in the
following:

(1) Open the equalizer valve.

(2)  Open the stop valve of low pressure side tapping-pipe and
the stop valve of low pressure side of manifold.

(3)  Open the stop valve of high pressure side tapping-pipe and
the stop valve of high pressure side of manifold.

(4) Close the equalizer valve.

INSPECTION AND MAINTENANCE

For routine inspection and maintenance, pay attention to the fol-
lowing:
1. Check for Leak from Piping

Check that there is no leak in the piping from the pressure tap points
to the meter body. If any loose connections are found, tighten them se-
curely.
2. Blow and Cleaning of Meter Body and Piping

To maintain constantly the instrument at its best performance meeting
its specification accuracy, keep clean the meter body and its piping. If
sediment or other foreign matters are entrapped in the pressure chambers of
the meter body, measuring errors may be caused. To blow and clean the meter
body and piping, proceed as mentioned in the following:
2.1 Blow Procedure

2.1.1 When No Blow Pipes are Provided

that of the high pressure side of the manifold valve.

(2)  Check that the equalizer valve is closed.

(3)  Close the stop valve of the low pressure side tapping pipe and

(4)  slowly open the both side vent plugs to release pressure. And
shut them.

(5) Open the stop valve of the high pressure side of the manifold
valve and that of the high pressure side tapping pipe.

(6) Open the equalizer and blow the piping via the high pressure
side vent plug.

(7)  Close the equalizer valve, the stop valve of the high pressure
side of the manifold valve, and that of the high pressure side
tapping pipe.

(8) Open the stop valve of the low pressure side of the manifold
valve and that of the low pressure side tapping pipe.

(9) Open the equalizer valve and blow the piping from the low
pressure side vent hole.

(10)  Close all valve.

(11)  start operating the instrument following the procedure explained
in Part "OPERATION METHOD."

the pilot relay to the guide hole, and tighten the screws evenly.

spring (5) to allow the solvent to penetrate through the seat

(e) Dry all parts thoroughly with clean compressed air.
(£)  Replace diaphragms (16) and (11) if worn or damaged.

25 General

The detector (meter body) feeds a torque, which is proportional to the
measured process variable, via the torque tube to the transmitting mechanism
Of the transmitter. Therefore, regardless of the type of the meter body,
the transmitter should be adjusted so that it transmits and output signal of
0.2 to 1.0 kgf/cm? linearly proportional to the 0% to 100% measuring range.

If the transmitter is incorporated with an elevation/suppression
spring assembly, remove the assembly in order to calibrate the measuring
range of the transmitter without zero-point shift.

Connect a precision pressure gauge of a range of approximately 0 to 1.5
kgf/cm? to the output port for calibration when checking the transmitter
operation or adjustment when replacing meter bodies or changing ranges.

a. Calibration of Differential Pressure Transmitter

(1) Release the low pressure connection port (marked L) to atmos-
phere.

(2) Connect to the high pressure connection port (marked H) a
variable pressure source (such as the air supply via a regu-
lator) corresponding to the measuring range and a precision
pressure gauge covering the measuring range. (For a flange type
meter body, prepare a piping adaptor.)

(3)  Apply to the high pressure connection port a pressure corre-
sponding to 100% of the measuring range by adjusting the vari-
able pressure source.

(4) If the output pressure is largely shifted from 100% (1.0
kgf/cm?), adjust the SPAN CHANGE boss using a plain screwdriver

turn the boss clockwise, the output pressure rises and the span
becomes narrower. As your turn the boss counterclockwise, the

(5)  Release the high pressure connection port to atmosphere.

(6)  If the output pressure is shifted from 0% (0.2 kgf/cm?), adjust
the ZERO ADJ screw so that the output pressure becomes 0%. As
you turn the screw clockwise, the output pressure rises, and

vice versa.

(7). Apply to the high pressure connection port a pressure corre-
sponding to 100% of the measuring range.

5. LAPPING AND REPAIR
The disc and seat of this safety valve can be easily lapped on
a lapping plate with use of a proper type of lapping compound.
As lapping compound is to be chosen # 600 kneaded with purified
rape oil ("shirashimeyu") .to non-flowing consistency.
Hints on lapping:
1. Both lapping plate and parts to be kept clean.
2. Excessive application of compound to be avoided.
3. Lapping to be performed with lapping plate or disc in an alter-
nate turning motion and pressed on lightly.
4. Lapping plate be precisely finished and kept well repaired.
5. Lapping of disc against seat to be avoided.
Practically, this valve calls for no repair work beyond lapping
on the part of users and should there be any parts calling for repair,

Precautions for Operation

1)

In the water level gauge sent out from our factory, there is a balance
kept between the bolt tightening force and the gas tightness of each
part by the hydraulic test under the ordinary temperature. Therefore,
when this instrument is installed to the boiler in operation and also
when the pressure and temperature are raised from the ordinary condi-
tions along with the boiler pressure increase, take care to avoid a
rapid pressure and temperature application to the water level gauge.

Disassembly and Reassembly of Water Level Gauge

After the lapse of the service'life, the prism surface of the reflex
type gauge glass wears out and loses reflectivity and as a result the
water level becomes invisible. As the gauge glass and sheet packings
are damaged, it becomes impossible to stop leakage with tightening of
the water level gauge bolts and nuts. In such a case, disassemble the
water level gauge and replace the worn-out parts in the following pro-
cedure:

1) Detach the water level gauge from the upper and lower cocks.
Disassemble the water level gauge preferably when it is still in
hot condition (this will facilitate the removal of the packings).

2) Place the water level gauge on a working bench, loosen the bolts
and nuts and remove the cover.

3) As the gauge glass is sticking to the level gauge body through
packings, slightly rap the side with a wooden hammer to remove the
glass. If this disassembly is made in an early time, the gauge
glass may be re-used if only the packings are replaced. In such
a case, therefore, remove the glass carefully not to break it

4) Remove the packings carefully not to damage the gas tightness of
the water level gauge body (the surface of contact with the packings).
Clean out the residual dirts.

be reversed. Follow the below instructions, referring to Figure A

b. As the sheet packing is hollowed out in the center part, it is

Replacement of Gland Packings

When the cap nut tightening cannot stop the leakage from the gauge cock
and level gauge connecting part, replace the gland packings, following
the below instructions:

1) Remove the upper and lower cap nuts and packing gland, and push up
the gland packing.

2) Disconnect the lower lead plpe from the lower cock, and extract the
upper lead pipe.

3) Smash the gland packings with use of a chisel, and remove them com-
pletely. Clean the stuffing box.

4) Drive in new gland packings to the position just lower than the
specified position of the upper lead pipe. Then, plunge the upper
lead pipe into the upper cock.

5) Gland packings must be replaced every time when the water level
gauge is disassembled. However, copper packings can be re-used
after they are annealed.

6) Then, carefully drive the gland packings into the stuffing box and
mount the packing gland and upper cap nut.

7) Tighten the cap nuts in the order of the lower cap nut and upper
cap nut.

Other Precautions

At periodical maintenance, disassemble the water level gauge and carry
out the replacement of consumables and the cleaning of the gas (water)
passage holes. Apply graphite paste to each threaded part before re-
assembly. Check the gas tight surface of the water level gauge for any
distortion or erosion. When necessary, repair or replace them.
