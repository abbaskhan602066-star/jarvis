1. Operation
1-1 Preparations for operation

Be sure to check and confirm the following items before starting operation.

(1) Cleaning:- HWipe off the dust, etc. from the motor; be sure to use a piece of dry
cloth. Never use wet or oil-stained cloth to wipe the electrically conductive
section.

(2) Tightening sections:- Check the motor bolts for loosening and detachment, paying
particular attention to the electrically conductive section,

(3) Terminals:- Check the lead wires and cables for scratched or cracked coatings as
well as the bolts for loosening.

(4) Insulation resistance:- Measure the insulation resistance between motor windings
and earth with a 500 V megger to make sure that the resistance is above the value

1-2 Check af ter start

After starting the motor, carry out no-load or lighi-load operation to check the

temperature, vibration. sound, etc. at each section.

(1} Abnormal noise:- Check each section for abnormal noise by using a sound
istening bar.
(2) Abnormal vibration:- Check each section for abnormal vibration by touching with
and or using a viorometer.
(3) Temperature rise in bearing:- Should the bearing be found to have excessive
temperature rise, install a thermometer to the bearing, and carry out inspection
of the bearing according to the item 2-2, if the temperature exceeds 45 degree.
1-3 Check during operation

Daily watch and check of the wachine during operation are extremery important; check

the following items.

(1) Noise:- Check each section for abnormal noise by using a sound listening bar.

(2) Odor:- Check for burning smell of varnish, oil, etc. to detect the abnormality.

(3) Temperature rise:- Confirm the temperature at each section by means of the

thermometer.
(4) Vibration:- Check each section for abnormal vibration by touching with hand or

using a vibrometer.

type ball bearing. A special duplex angular contact ball bearing (DB type) is used on

from vibration. Carry out the maintenance of bearing according to the maintenance

procedures given helow.

2-4 Ttems of caution when the motor is at rest

It is desirable that the motor winding and insulator are always clean and dry.

Take utmost care of humidity when the motor is at rest.

In case the motor is not used over a long period, cover the whole motor unit with

waterproof sheet and firmly fix with tape or rope and keep the motor in nearly

enclosed (seai up) state in order to prevent the intrusion of moisture, dust, efc.

Furthermore, connect to the space heater in order to prevent moisture absorption and

perspriring of winding and bearing of the motor. However, if the space heater is not

installed, put moisture absorbing agenis such as silica-gel, etc. into the water

proof sheet before sealing.

Since the grease, when left unused over a long period. has the oil component separated,

causing the grease to get hardened and affected, carry out hand turning of the rotor

once every two months when the grease is stored or the motor is not operated for a long

time,

2-5 Drying of winding coil

In case the insulation resistance gets decreased due to moisture absorption by the

insulator, and drying is necessary, be sure to thoroughly clean the whole motor unit

including the winding coil before drying.

(1) Cover the whole motor unit with the sheet and blow in hot air by using a hot air

drying machine, taking care so that the hot air may not come directly on the winding
coil and shaft to cause them to get locally heated. See to it that the maximum drying
temperature is approximately 90~100°C, and install thermometer to carry out
temperature control,

When covering the sheet, provide proper space to enable thorough circulation of

hot air,

(Do not use coke, coal gas or kerosene stove as the heat source.)

Measure the insulation resistance at a certain interval while drying, and carry

out drying for over 24 hours, after the insulation resistance shows a constant
level. The change of insulation resistance measured and recorded during is shown

in Fig.2. Make sure that the value has reached the saturation point.

4. Disassembly and Assembly

4-1 General cautions

Below are the general procedures and items of caution for disassembly and assembly

motor. Be sure to read them thoroughly before starting the disassembly/assembly.

(1) Disassembly or assembly by one person is not only inefficient but is likely to

cause fatal damage to the machine,

Hence, see to it that the job is carried out by two or more pcople.

(2) Keep the place of disassembly in good order and well arranged to prevent

mixing up of other parts and to improve the work efficiency.

(3) Cover the disassembled parts, particularly the important rotating parts such

as bearing, etc.,with vinyl or cloth to protect from dust.

4-2 Disassembly

Before pulling out or inserting the rotor from or into the stator, fit their
shaft centers so that the two may not come in contact. Take particular care so
as not to scratch the stator winding coil, rotor bar, shortcircuit ring, core,
etc.

Lay the rotor on a stand, and be sure to cover with vinyl or cloth, and wrap
the bearing part of shaft with cloth for storage.

When separating the fan and fan boss, be sure to put fitting marks, and use
these marks at the time of reassembly.

As shown in the Bearing construction in Fig.l, the bearing cover has the
groove for the grease to pass through.

At the time of assembly, pay due attention to the position of the groove.
Erronecusly located groove causes failure in grease circulation, making the

grease exchange impossible (not required for sealed type ball bearing).

See the sectional drawings of the motor carefully to get the outline of the

construction before starting disassembly.

The sectional drawings of the motor are shown in figs.3~7.
